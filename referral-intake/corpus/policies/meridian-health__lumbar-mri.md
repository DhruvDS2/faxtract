# Meridian Health — Medical Policy: MRI Lumbar Spine (CPT 72148)

Meridian Health considers MRI of the lumbar spine medically necessary when the referral documents:
- Low back pain or lumbar radiculopathy persisting at least 6 weeks despite conservative treatment (physical therapy, NSAIDs), or
- Progressive neurologic deficit or motor weakness, or
- Red-flag features suggesting malignancy, infection, or cauda equina syndrome.

Prior authorization is required for all MRI studies. Required documentation: clinical notes and prior imaging.
Submit via the Meridian provider portal. Standard turnaround is 3 business days.
