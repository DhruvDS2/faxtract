# Northgate Plan — Medical Policy: CT Upper Extremity (CPT 73200)

Policy No. NRTH-73200 · Review cycle: annual · All content synthetic.

## Background
CT of an upper extremity evaluates complex osseous anatomy where radiographs are insufficient. It is directed at bony detail rather than soft-tissue structures.

## Definitions
- **intra-articular fracture**: a fracture extending into the joint surface
- **osseous detail**: fine bony architecture not resolved on plain radiographs

## Coverage Criteria
Northgate Plan considers ct upper extremity medically necessary when the referral documents any of the following:
- A complex or intra-articular fracture not adequately characterized on radiographs
- Suspected bone tumor, osteomyelitis, or aggressive osseous lesion
- Postoperative evaluation of hardware, nonunion, or malunion
The requested study must specify laterality (right or left).

## Documentation Requirements
The referral must include clinical notes, prior imaging of the same region, and a signed conservative-treatment attestation confirming that guideline-concordant non-surgical care was trialed and documented. Incomplete submissions are returned without a coverage determination.

## Non-Covered and Exclusions
Northgate Plan does not consider the study medically necessary in the following circumstances:
- A simple non-displaced fracture adequately seen on radiographs
- Soft-tissue evaluation better suited to MRI or ultrasound

## Exceptions and Expedited Review
An open fracture, or suspected compartment syndrome, warrants expedited review.

## Prior Authorization and Submission
Prior authorization is REQUIRED for this study. Required documentation: clinical notes, prior imaging, and a signed conservative-treatment attestation.
Submit via fax. Standard turnaround is 2 business days.
