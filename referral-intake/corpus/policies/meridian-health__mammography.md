# Meridian Health — Medical Policy: Diagnostic Mammography (CPT 77066)

Meridian Health considers diagnostic mammography medically necessary when the referral documents:
- A palpable breast lump or other focal breast symptom, or
- Further evaluation of an abnormal or inconclusive screening finding, or
- Short-interval follow-up of a probably-benign prior finding.

Prior authorization is not required for mammography. Clinical notes should accompany the order.
