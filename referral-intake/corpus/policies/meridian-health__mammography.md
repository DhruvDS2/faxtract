# Meridian Health — Medical Policy: Diagnostic Mammography (CPT 77066)

Policy No. MRDN-77066 · Review cycle: annual · All content synthetic.

## Background
Diagnostic mammography evaluates a specific breast concern and is distinct from routine screening mammography. It is directed by a focal symptom or an abnormal prior study.

## Definitions
- **focal breast symptom**: a palpable lump, focal pain, nipple discharge, or localized skin change
- **short-interval follow-up**: repeat imaging of a probably-benign finding at a defined interval

## Coverage Criteria
Meridian Health considers diagnostic mammography medically necessary when the referral documents any of the following:
- A palpable breast lump or other focal breast symptom
- Further evaluation of an abnormal or inconclusive screening finding
- Short-interval follow-up of a probably-benign prior finding
- Evaluation in a patient with a personal history of breast cancer

## Documentation Requirements
The referral must include clinical notes documenting the presenting complaint, the duration of symptoms, and any prior conservative management. Prior imaging of the same region, if available, must be submitted for comparison.

## Non-Covered and Exclusions
Meridian Health does not consider the study medically necessary in the following circumstances:
- Routine annual screening in an asymptomatic average-risk patient (billed under screening codes)

## Exceptions and Expedited Review
A clinically suspicious mass with associated skin changes warrants expedited diagnostic evaluation.

## Prior Authorization and Submission
Prior authorization is NOT required for MG studies under this plan. Clinical notes should accompany the order.
Submit via the Meridian provider portal. Standard turnaround is 3 business days.
