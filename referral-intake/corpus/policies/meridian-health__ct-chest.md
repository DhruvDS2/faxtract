# Meridian Health — Medical Policy: CT Chest (CPT 71260)

Meridian Health considers CT of the chest medically necessary when the referral documents:
- Follow-up of a solitary pulmonary nodule identified on prior imaging, or
- Chronic cough or hemoptysis with clinical suspicion of malignancy, or
- Characterization or staging of known or suspected thoracic disease or lymphadenopathy.

Prior authorization is required for CT studies. Required documentation: clinical notes and prior imaging.
Submit via the Meridian provider portal. Standard turnaround is 3 business days.
