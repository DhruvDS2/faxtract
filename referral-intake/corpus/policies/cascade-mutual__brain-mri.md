# Cascade Mutual — Medical Policy: MRI Brain (CPT 70553)

Cascade Mutual considers MRI of the brain medically necessary when the referral documents:
- Persistent or worsening headache with red-flag features, or
- Focal neurologic deficit, seizure, or suspected intracranial mass, or
- Unexplained dizziness or vertigo after initial evaluation.

Prior authorization is required for MRI studies. Required documentation: clinical notes.
Submit by fax. Standard turnaround is 5 business days.
