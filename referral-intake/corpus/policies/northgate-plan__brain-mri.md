# Northgate Plan — Medical Policy: MRI Brain (CPT 70553)

Northgate Plan considers MRI of the brain medically necessary when the referral documents:
- New, worsening, or thunderclap headache with red-flag features, OR
- Focal neurologic deficit, seizure, or suspected intracranial mass or tumor, OR
- Unexplained dizziness or vertigo after a documented initial workup.

Prior authorization is required for all advanced imaging. Required documentation: clinical notes, prior imaging, and a conservative-treatment attestation.
Submit by fax. Standard turnaround is 2 business days.
