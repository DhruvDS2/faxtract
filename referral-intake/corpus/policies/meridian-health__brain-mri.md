# Meridian Health — Medical Policy: MRI Brain (CPT 70553)

Meridian Health considers MRI of the brain medically necessary when the referral documents:
- New, worsening, or thunderclap headache with red-flag features, or
- Focal neurologic deficit, seizure, or suspected intracranial mass or tumor, or
- Unexplained dizziness or vertigo after an initial clinical workup.

Prior authorization is required for all MRI studies. Required documentation: clinical notes and prior imaging.
Submit via the Meridian provider portal. Standard turnaround is 3 business days.
