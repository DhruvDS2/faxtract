# Cascade Mutual — Medical Policy: CT Chest (CPT 71260)

Cascade Mutual considers CT of the chest medically necessary when the referral documents:
- Follow-up of a pulmonary nodule seen on prior imaging, or
- Chronic cough or hemoptysis with suspicion of malignancy, or
- Evaluation of known or suspected thoracic disease or lymphadenopathy.

Prior authorization is not required for CT studies under this plan. Clinical notes should accompany the order.
Submit by fax if authorization is later requested. Standard turnaround is 5 business days.
