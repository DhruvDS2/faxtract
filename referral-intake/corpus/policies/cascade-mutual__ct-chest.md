# Cascade Mutual — Medical Policy: CT Chest (CPT 71260)

Policy No. CASC-71260 · Review cycle: annual · All content synthetic.

## Background
CT of the chest with contrast evaluates the lung parenchyma, mediastinum, and thoracic vasculature. It is used to characterize findings seen on prior imaging or to investigate symptoms suspicious for malignancy or thoracic disease.

## Definitions
- **pulmonary nodule**: a discrete rounded opacity up to 3 cm identified on prior imaging
- **structured follow-up**: interval imaging performed on a recognized nodule-surveillance schedule

## Coverage Criteria
Cascade Mutual considers ct chest medically necessary when the referral documents any of the following:
- Structured follow-up of a pulmonary nodule identified on prior imaging
- Chronic cough or hemoptysis with clinical suspicion of malignancy
- Evaluation of a known or suspected thoracic mass, lymphadenopathy, or interstitial disease
- Staging of a known malignancy

## Documentation Requirements
The referral must include clinical notes describing the presenting complaint and the clinical rationale for the requested study. Additional records may be requested during review.

## Non-Covered and Exclusions
Cascade Mutual does not consider the study medically necessary in the following circumstances:
- Routine screening outside an approved lung-cancer screening program
- Acute uncomplicated upper-respiratory infection

## Exceptions and Expedited Review
Massive hemoptysis, or suspected pulmonary embolism with hemodynamic compromise, warrants expedited review.

## Prior Authorization and Submission
Prior authorization is NOT required for CT studies under this plan. Clinical notes should accompany the order.
Submit via fax. Standard turnaround is 5 business days.
