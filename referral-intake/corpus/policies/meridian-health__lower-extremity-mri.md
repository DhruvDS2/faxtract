# Meridian Health — Medical Policy: MRI Lower Extremity Joint (CPT 73721)

Policy No. MRDN-73721 · Review cycle: annual · All content synthetic.

## Background
MRI of a lower-extremity joint, most commonly the knee, evaluates internal derangement of the cartilage, menisci, ligaments, and subchondral bone when radiographs and clinical examination are insufficient.

## Definitions
- **internal derangement**: a structural injury such as a meniscal or ligament tear
- **mechanical symptoms**: locking, catching, or the joint giving way
- **conservative management**: a documented trial of physical therapy, NSAIDs, and activity modification

## Coverage Criteria
Meridian Health considers mri lower extremity joint medically necessary when the referral documents any of the following:
- Persistent joint pain or mechanical symptoms lasting at least 6 weeks despite conservative management
- Suspected internal derangement (meniscal or ligament tear) following acute injury
- Suspected osteoarthritis, avascular necrosis, or occult fracture not explained by radiographs
The requested study must specify laterality (right or left).

## Documentation Requirements
The referral must include clinical notes documenting the presenting complaint, the duration of symptoms, and any prior conservative management. Prior imaging of the same region, if available, must be submitted for comparison.

## Non-Covered and Exclusions
Meridian Health does not consider the study medically necessary in the following circumstances:
- Acute uncomplicated joint sprain without mechanical symptoms
- Repeat imaging absent a change in clinical status

## Exceptions and Expedited Review
A locked knee, or suspected complete ligament rupture with instability, warrants expedited review without the conservative-management waiting period.

## Prior Authorization and Submission
Prior authorization is REQUIRED for this study. Required documentation: clinical notes and prior imaging.
Submit via the Meridian provider portal. Standard turnaround is 3 business days.
