# Cascade Mutual — Medical Policy: MRI Lumbar Spine (CPT 72148)

Cascade Mutual considers MRI of the lumbar spine medically necessary when the referral documents:
- Persistent low back pain or lumbar radiculopathy not improved after a course of conservative care, or
- Neurologic deficit, or
- Suspicion of malignancy, infection, or cauda equina syndrome.

Prior authorization is required for MRI studies. Required documentation: clinical notes.
Submit by fax. Standard turnaround is 5 business days.
