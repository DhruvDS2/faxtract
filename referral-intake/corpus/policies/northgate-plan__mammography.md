# Northgate Plan — Medical Policy: Diagnostic Mammography (CPT 77066)

Northgate Plan considers diagnostic mammography medically necessary when the referral documents:
- A palpable breast lump or focal breast symptom, OR
- Evaluation of an abnormal or inconclusive screening finding, OR
- Short-interval follow-up of a probably-benign prior finding.

Prior authorization is not required for mammography. Clinical notes should accompany the order.
