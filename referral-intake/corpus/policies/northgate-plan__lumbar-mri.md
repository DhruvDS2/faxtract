# Northgate Plan — Medical Policy: MRI Lumbar Spine (CPT 72148)

Northgate Plan considers MRI of the lumbar spine medically necessary when the referral documents:
- Low back pain or lumbar radiculopathy persisting at least 6 weeks, AND
- A documented trial of conservative treatment (physical therapy and NSAIDs), OR
- Progressive neurologic deficit or red-flag features (malignancy, infection, cauda equina).

Prior authorization is required for all advanced imaging. Required documentation: clinical notes, prior imaging, and a conservative-treatment attestation.
Submit by fax. Standard turnaround is 2 business days.
