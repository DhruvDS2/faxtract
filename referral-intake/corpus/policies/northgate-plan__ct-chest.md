# Northgate Plan — Medical Policy: CT Chest (CPT 71260)

Northgate Plan considers CT of the chest medically necessary when the referral documents:
- Follow-up of a solitary pulmonary nodule on prior imaging, OR
- Chronic cough or hemoptysis with clinical suspicion of malignancy, OR
- Characterization or staging of known or suspected thoracic disease or lymphadenopathy.

Prior authorization is required for all advanced imaging. Required documentation: clinical notes, prior imaging, and a conservative-treatment attestation.
Submit by fax. Standard turnaround is 2 business days.
