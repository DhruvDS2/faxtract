# Northgate Plan — Medical Policy: Ultrasound Abdomen Complete (CPT 76700)

Policy No. NRTH-76700 · Review cycle: annual · All content synthetic.

## Background
Complete abdominal ultrasound evaluates the liver, gallbladder, biliary tree, kidneys, pancreas, and spleen without ionizing radiation. It is the preferred first-line study for suspected hepatobiliary disease.

## Definitions
- **RUQ pain**: right upper quadrant pain suggestive of biliary disease
- **hepatobiliary**: relating to the liver and biliary system

## Coverage Criteria
Northgate Plan considers ultrasound abdomen complete medically necessary when the referral documents any of the following:
- Right upper quadrant pain with suspected gallstones or cholecystitis
- Abnormal liver function tests or suspected hepatobiliary disease
- Evaluation of hepatomegaly, ascites, or a known or suspected abdominal mass
- Surveillance of a known abdominal aortic aneurysm

## Documentation Requirements
The referral must include clinical notes, prior imaging of the same region, and a signed conservative-treatment attestation confirming that guideline-concordant non-surgical care was trialed and documented. Incomplete submissions are returned without a coverage determination.

## Non-Covered and Exclusions
Northgate Plan does not consider the study medically necessary in the following circumstances:
- Routine screening in asymptomatic average-risk patients (except approved aneurysm screening)

## Exceptions and Expedited Review
Suspected acute cholecystitis with systemic signs warrants expedited evaluation.

## Prior Authorization and Submission
Prior authorization is NOT required for US studies under this plan. Clinical notes should accompany the order.
Submit via fax. Standard turnaround is 2 business days.
